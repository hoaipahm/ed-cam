# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- Centered swipe zoom tabs, fine zoom ruler, four photo lighting choices, video torch and camera capability details.
- Reusable color recipes, three-look previews and framing from an earlier photo.
- GPU color matrix during MP4 encoding and the same intensity in the viewfinder.
- Native JPEG sharing and optional self-hosted HTTPS links with color votes and revocation.

## 1.3.0

- Replaced WebView control replicas with native buttons, switches, sliders, dialogs and ripple feedback. Original effect assets remain unchanged in source.
- Rebuilt the camera with a large shutter, equal-width tools, Photo/Video modes, hardware zoom stops and pinch-to-zoom.
- Added exposure compensation and hardware-aware lens/zoom selection, including zoom-out when exposed by Camera2.
- Added private MP4 recording with optional audio, pause/resume, finalization when leaving the camera, playback, deletion and MediaStore export.
- Compacted theme selection and grouped settings. Preserved presets, favorites, private ratings, import, originals and photo export.
- Invalidated stale timer callbacks, persisted timer choices, mapped focus through preview transforms and replaced the full-screen progress overlay with a slim indicator.
- Added emulator integration, native touch and large-text visual checks. Physical camera/flash/zoom/performance acceptance remains separate.

## 1.2.2

- Reworked the viewfinder controls with a darker toolbar, compact mode tabs, and a circular shutter.

## 1.2.1

- Kept camera labels compact at larger text sizes and made the star rating fit narrow layouts.
- Moved Sakuga particles behind controls and removed duplicate capture notifications.

## 1.2.0

- Expanded the preset library from 60 to 180 while preserving the original preset IDs.
- Added original-color comparison, private photo ratings, and the full set of Sakuga gallery controls.
- Updated Camera2 flash capture with a bounded exposure preparation step. Physical flash remains unverified.

## 1.1.0

- Integrated the original Sakuga tabs with native camera and app navigation.
- The downloadable APK was rebuilt from commit `fdf5893`.

## 1.0.2

- Enlarged the full-screen camera viewfinder and reorganized capture controls.

## 1.0.1

- First archived APK for the personal camera and photo editor.
